# Browse and saved recipes

Mode: Operate. Target: `src/App.svelte`, recipe cards, recipe and grocery sheets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A neighborhood market makes food and the facts needed to choose it immediately visible. Flat photographs and a time–publisher strip replace the mounted index-card metaphor.

OWN-WORLD: Deep market green, cool pale green ground, white food surfaces, yellow for the random-pick action. Barlow Semi Condensed titles and DM Sans controls; no handwritten labels, tilted stickers or decorative stalls.

STORY: Search or narrow by time and meal, compare food, time and credited publisher, open ingredients, then visit the creator. Saved recipes and shopping remain familiar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact green header, visible language link and search; quick filters followed by quiet category toggles. Two recipe columns on phones, each with photo, title and a stable time–publisher strip. Yellow random-pick action within thumb reach. Scroll reveals separate return-to-top and filter controls.

FORM: Neighborhood market signage, candidate 1, chosen by the user over the assigned candidate; seed:66c9f34e862e847d. Code-led. The approved sketch is `.impeccable/mocks/decision/mercato.html`, a composition reference rather than a pixel contract.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
