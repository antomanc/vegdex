## Decided
Landing pages for search (#75) are kept small on purpose: search is a low priority for vegdex, and the feature should add little code. One page per meal tag plus 24 hand-picked headline ingredients, in each UI language, generated by the Vite SEO plugin from the emitted catalog and listed in the sitemap with hreflang pairs. Nothing generated is committed. Each page lists the 60 most-rated recipe titles; links open the recipe or a filtered view in the app.

## Tried and ruled out
Picking ingredients by recipe count: the top concepts are salt, water, pepper and oil, and the dictionary's names are messy ("good pinch of ground cloves"), so the page names would be junk. A hand list with a name per language is shorter and reads right.
Showing the recipe count on the page: the app's `have=` filter also matches ingredient text, so it shows more recipes (1329 vs 751 for chickpeas) and the numbers disagreed.

## Watch out
The build reads whatever catalog sits in public/data, so local e2e and CI built different sitemaps. Playwright builds with LANDING_DATA pointing at the e2e fixtures, which have too few recipes for any page (pages need at least 10), so the sitemap test still sees only the two home pages.
Landing pages are excluded from the service worker's navigation fallback and precache; otherwise a returning visitor would get the app shell instead of the page.
